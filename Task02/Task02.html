Part 1: Phishing Emails

Phishing emails trick users into revealing passwords or sensitive information.

✅ Legitimate Email


From: support@company.com

Subject: Password Reset

Hello Sarah,

Visit: https://company.com

  • Professional language
  • HTTPS secure link
  • No threats

❌ Fake Email


From: support@cornpany-security.com

Subject: ACCOUNT LOCKED!

Verify your password immediately.

http://fake-login.net

  • Urgent language
  • Misspelled domain
  • Suspicious link

Part 2: Fake Websites

🌐 Safe Website


https://company.com

✔ HTTPS Enabled

⚠ Fake Website


http://cornpany-login.net

❌ No HTTPS

Part 3: Social Engineering

👔 Authority

Fake CEO or HR messages.

⏰ Urgency

Act now before account suspension.

🎁 Greed

Free rewards and gifts.

Quiz Time

Which is the biggest phishing warning sign?

  • A. Friendly greeting
  • B. Urgent password request
  • C. Company logo
✅ Correct Answer: B

Legitimate organizations do not pressure users to share passwords urgently.
